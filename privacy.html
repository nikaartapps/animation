Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Animatya is a frame-by-frame animation app for iPad. This policy explains how the app handles information. In short: it doesn’t collect any.

Data we collect

None. Animatya does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that gather information about you.

Your drawings and projects

Everything you create stays on your device, stored by the app in its own storage on your iPad. We have no access to it and never receive a copy. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your device backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Content you export (images, video, or project files) leaves the app only when you choose to save or share it, and goes wherever you send it.

Contacting support

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. It is used only to answer you, is never shared or sold, and is deleted when the conversation is no longer needed.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: nika+animatya@animatya.com
